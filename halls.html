Our Cinema Halls & Technologies

Discover our high-tech screening rooms built for acoustic perfection, maximum immersion, and unmatched comfort.

Hall 1: IMAX Laser Auditorium

Featuring a 22-meter floor-to-ceiling curved screen and next-generation dual 4K laser projection delivering vibrant colors and deep blacks.

Key Technical Specifications:

  1. Dual 4K Laser Commercial Projectors with HDR support
  2. 12-channel customized IMAX sound system with overhead ceiling speakers
  3. Acoustically tuned geometry with zero blind spots from any seat

Hall 2: VIP Premium Lounge

An intimate screening room designed for top-tier comfort. Enjoy private viewing with premium amenities and attentive service.

Exclusive Features:

  1. Motorized Italian leather recliners with USB charging ports
  2. Personal waiter call button built directly into the armrest
  3. Complimentary blanket, pillow, and food delivery during the movie

Hall 3: Dolby Atmos Soundstage

Engineered specifically for complete spatial audio immersion. Sound objects move freely around you in three-dimensional space.

Acoustic Architecture:

  1. 64 independent audio channels creating true 3D surround sound
  2. Subwoofers integrated beneath the floor for tactile low-frequency effects
  3. High-density sound-dampening acoustic wall panels
